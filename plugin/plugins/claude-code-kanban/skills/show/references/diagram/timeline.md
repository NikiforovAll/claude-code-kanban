# Timeline

For events in time: releases, milestones, an incident, a roadmap.

- On a card the axis runs down: a hairline at the left, dates in mono to its left, events to its right. A horizontal axis fits only 4 or 5 short events.
- An event is a filled dot, `r` 4, on the axis, with its label beside it. A milestone is an accent dot, `r` 6, with a bold label.
- Space events by real time. If one stretch is crowded and another empty, break the axis with a visible gap mark rather than fake even spacing.
- Name the unit of the axis (days, sprints, versions).
- An incident timeline can color its dots by status (`--color-danger` for the outage start, `--color-success` for the recovery), and nothing else.
